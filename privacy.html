InkStage Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: InkStage does not collect, sell or share any information about you. Your files, writing and notes stay on your iPad. If you choose to back up to Google Drive, your files go straight from your iPad to your own Google Drive. They are never sent to the maker of InkStage.

Who makes InkStage

InkStage is an iPad app made by Jason Renner, a teacher, for presenting slides, PDFs and web pages and writing on them with Apple Pencil. Questions about this policy can be sent to jason.renner1@gmail.com.

What InkStage collects

Nothing. InkStage has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not run its own servers, so there is nowhere for your information to be sent.

What stays on your iPad

Everything you put in InkStage is kept on your iPad, in the app's own storage:

  • the files you import or create (HTML pages, PDFs, documents, canvases),
  • your Apple Pencil writing, text boxes, pictures and presenter notes,
  • your settings, sounds and Library folders.

This information may be included in your iPad's own iCloud or computer backups, under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting InkStage removes it from your iPad. A Backup & Restore file you make yourself goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Class tools and student rosters

InkStage includes classroom tools (Student Look Up, Group Maker, Popsicle Sticks, Name Game and Classroom Announcer). If you load a class roster into them, it can include students' names, photos, grades and notes such as learning or seating needs. This information:

  • is stored only on your iPad, inside InkStage;
  • is never sent to the maker of InkStage, to Google or to anyone else. The class tools work completely offline and do not connect to the internet;
  • is not included in Google Drive backups or Backup & Restore files, unless you yourself export or back up a page that shows it;
  • can be removed at any time by deleting it in the class tool, deleting the tool from your Library, or deleting the InkStage app.

The Roster Maker on the InkStage Google Site builds the roster file inside your own web browser. The photos and names you add are not uploaded, sent or saved anywhere: the page makes the file on your computer and saves it to your Downloads folder, and everything on the page is cleared when you close it. Where you keep the roster file afterwards (for example, your school Google Drive) is your choice. That site is hosted by Google Sites, so Google may keep ordinary visit logs under Google's privacy policy.

The teacher who loads a roster is responsible for following their school's rules about student information.

Google Drive backup (optional)

InkStage can back up your files to your own Google Drive. This is off until you choose to use it and sign in with your Google account. Here is exactly how InkStage handles Google user data:

  • Data accessed: InkStage asks Google only for the drive.file permission. This lets it create files in your Google Drive and update the files it created. It cannot see, open, read, change or delete any other file in your Google Drive. It does not ask for your contacts, email, calendar or any other Google data.
  • How it is used: only to back up your InkStage files. InkStage uploads a PDF copy of each file you back up (with your writing on it) to a folder called "InkStage Backups" in your Drive, and replaces that copy with a newer one each time it backs the file up again. If you turn on automatic backup for a file, this happens a short time after you change it.
  • Where it goes and what is stored: the backup goes straight from your iPad to Google over a secure (encrypted) connection. It never passes through any server run by the maker of InkStage, and the maker cannot see it. On your iPad, InkStage keeps your Google sign-in key in the iPad's secure Keychain, and the ID of each backup file so it can replace it next time. Nothing else from Google is stored.
  • Sharing: InkStage does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to develop, improve or train any artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Keeping and deleting: backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them there. To stop backups, use ⋯ → Sign Out of Google Drive in InkStage, which removes the sign-in key from your iPad. You can also remove InkStage's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

InkStage's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Websites and videos

InkStage has a built-in web browser. When you open a website in it, play a YouTube video in a slide, or open a file that loads content from the internet, your iPad connects directly to that website or to YouTube, just as it would in Safari. Those sites' own privacy policies apply to what they collect.

  • InkStage keeps no list of the websites you visit and no record of what you search for.
  • Like any browser, it keeps websites' cookies and saved data on your iPad, so you stay signed in to sites you use. This is kept only on your iPad, separate from your files, and is never sent to the maker of InkStage.
  • You can delete it at any time: open the website box (Library → Browser, or ⋯ → Open a Website) and tap Clear Browser Data.
  • Writing you do on a website is not saved.

Trouble-shooting log

To help fix problems, InkStage keeps a short technical log on your iPad (for example "page opened" or an error message). It does not contain website addresses or search words. It never leaves your iPad unless you choose to send it to someone.

Other companies

InkStage contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code from other companies. The only other companies involved are the ones you choose to use:

  • Google, if you turn on Google Drive backup (see above), under Google's privacy policy.
  • Websites and YouTube that you open or that a file loads, under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple, which may include InkStage's data in your iPad's iCloud or computer backups, under Apple's privacy policy.

The maker of InkStage receives no information from any of them about you.

Keeping your information safe

Your files stay in InkStage's own protected storage on your iPad. Your Google sign-in key is kept in the iPad's Keychain. Backups to Google Drive are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Your choices and deleting your information

  • Delete any file in InkStage's Library (deleted files stay in the Trash for 30 days, then are removed).
  • Clear website data with Clear Browser Data, and stop Google Drive backups with Sign Out of Google Drive.
  • Deleting the InkStage app removes everything it stored on your iPad.

Because the maker of InkStage never receives or keeps any of your information, there is nothing held by the maker to request or delete. If you have a question, use the contact address below.

Children and students

InkStage is a tool for teachers and presenters. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children and students. Anything a teacher writes or shows in InkStage stays on the teacher's iPad (and in the teacher's own Google Drive, if they turn on backup).
