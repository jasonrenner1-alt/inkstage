InkStage

Present and write on your lessons with Apple Pencil.

InkStage is an iPad app made by a teacher for teaching. Open HTML slide decks, interactive web pages and PDFs, write on them with Apple Pencil, and show them on a projector or TV with AirPlay.

  • Write, highlight, type and draw shapes on any slide or page
  • Show your lesson on the projector while your notes stay on the iPad
  • Class tools: timer, calculator with graphing, blank pages and grid paper
  • Export your lesson with your writing as a PDF, or back it up to your own Google Drive
